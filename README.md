# DÉLICE — Styling Atelier, Couture & Ready-to-Wear

> **High-fashion styling studio and bespoke atelier in Lévis, Quebec.**  
> Built with an editorial **Lavender Pink** aesthetic, gentle pastel containers, non-glaring restful typography, and art direction powered by **`taste-skill`**.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Color Palette

The visual direction replaces generic templates with deliberate architectural scale and high-fashion aesthetics:

* **Background Pop of Color**:
  * An electric yet warm **Radiant Velvet Lavender Pink** gradient (`#e06bb2` &rarr; `#b8418c` &rarr; `#8e276b`) with ambient glowing lilac lighting.
* **Mild Surface Colors**:
  * **Whipped Lilac Cream** (`#faf5f8`) & **Heather Linen** (`#f4eaf2`)
  * **Powder Lavender Mist** (`#ede0f4`)
  * **Cashmere Powder Pink** (`#fcebf2`) & **Petal Rose** (`#f7e4ee`)
  * **Subdued Herbal Tea Sage** (`#e1ede5`) & **Champagne Butter** (`#fcf4ea`)
* **Restful, Non-Eye-Pointing Typography**:
  * **Velvety Plum Espresso** (`#251726`): Deep, glare-free, highly readable text.
  * **Mulberry Mocha** (`#5c445e`): Gentle secondary captions and descriptions.
  * **Soft Dusty Mauve** (`#856a87`): Refined metadata and labels.
  * **Font Pairing**: `Syne` (Parisian modern fashion display) + `Italiana` (editorial serif) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (clean readability).

## ✨ Features & Sections

Adapted from the signature daily rituals and hospitality of Délice:

1. **Top Daily Ticker**:
   * Continuous rolling marquee displaying weekly privileges and specials (*Happy Hour*, *Lunch Specials*, *Vintage Tuesday*, *Wednesdélice*, *Silhouette Lab*, *Wine & Suites*).
2. **Hero Statement**:
   * Architectural headline (*"The art of wardrobe, the daring of style."*), quick ritual pills, and bespoke badges.
3. **The Wardrobe Philosophy**:
   * Three mild pastel cards highlighting *Mild & Responsible Fabrics*, *Architectural Cuts & Ease*, and *Private Styling Sessions*.
4. **Daily Style Rituals ("Plaisirs du jour")**:
   * **Happy Hour Styling** (Daily 2 PM – 6 PM): Express silhouette consultation with lounge refreshments.
   * **Lunch Specials** (Daily 11 AM – 3 PM): 30-minute express 3-outfit midday capsule fitting.
   * **Archival Vintage Tuesday** (Tuesdays 11 AM – 10 PM): Curated drops of restored 70s/80s trench coats and blazers.
   * **Wednesdélice Concierge**: 20% off styled home delivery and courier try-on services.
   * **Adjustments & Unlimited Fitting** (Wednesdays 4 PM – 10 PM): Complimentary tailoring, hem adjustments, and shaping.
   * **Wine Tasting & VIP Suites** (Thursdays from 11 AM): Half-price private fitting suites with curated wine tastings.
5. **Key Heritage Stats**:
   * Interactive animated counters: **160** Passionate Artisans &bull; **1970** Founding Year &bull; **700** Wardrobes Curated per Season &bull; **300** Partner Designers.
6. **Filterable Lookbook**:
   * Interactive category filtering (*All Looks*, *Tailoring & Blazers*, *Casual Capsule*, *Evening & Cocktail*) featuring real editorial photography:
     * **Lavender Orchid Tailored Suit** ($440)
     * **Lévis Flânerie Pastel Capsule** ($340)
     * **VIP Suite Evening Smoking** ($560)
7. **VIP Private Fitting Suites**:
   * Dedicated fitting salons with champagne service, 360° day/evening lighting, and dedicated personal stylists.
8. **Careers / Open Positions ("On engage")**:
   * Complete interactive application form with role selection, drag-and-drop resume upload preview, and terms acceptance.
9. **Flagship Atelier & Location**:
   * Flagship address at **146 route du Président Kennedy, Lévis, QC**, interactive Google Maps card, live opening hours status badge, and at-home concierge request.
10. **Interactive Modals**:
    * **✦ Style Diagnostic Quiz**: 3-step interactive questionnaire providing personalized silhouette profiles.
    * **Appointment Booking**: Service pre-selection with real-time feedback.
    * **♫ Atelier Lounge Sound**: Web Audio API synthesizer creating gentle ambient analog jazz lounge chords.
    * **Bilingual Toggle (EN / FR)**: Real-time dynamic language switcher.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **HTML5**: Semantic markup, accessible aria attributes, and clean modal structures.
* **Vanilla CSS**: Custom properties (CSS variables), glassmorphism, responsive grid & flexbox layouts, smooth keyframe animations.
* **Vanilla JavaScript**: State management, multi-step quiz flow, Web Audio synthesizer, bilingual translation engine, and form handlers.
* **Art Direction**: Powered by [`taste-skill`](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill) anti-slop guidelines.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Run Locally

Simply serve the project folder using any static HTTP server:

```bash
# Using Python 3
python3 -m http.server 4180

# Or using Node
npx serve .
```

Open your browser at `http://localhost:4180`.
